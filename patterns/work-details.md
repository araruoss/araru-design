# Work details

Hierarchy: cover, title, authors, series, progress, primary reading action, description and metadata. Secondary actions stay quiet.

On expanded screens the cover may sit beside the content. On compact screens it may be centered above the title. The primary action is `Continue Reading` when progress exists, otherwise `Start Reading`; Favorite and More are secondary actions. Series is a navigable relationship with name, volume and other volumes—not only text metadata.

If a work has multiple files, present the work once and expose formats/files as selectable sources (PDF, EPUB, MOBI, CBZ or CBR). Loading, unavailable content, missing cover, permission failure and retry states are explicit.
