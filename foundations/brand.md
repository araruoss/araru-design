# Brand

Araru is a personal library: editorial, welcoming, quiet, organized and curious. The macaw/Cerrado reference is subtle—an accent, illustration or naming detail—not a theme applied to every screen.


Use “Araru” as the product name, never as a generic noun. Do not redraw or alter the existing logo without a dedicated brand decision. Prefer neutral surfaces, Deep Navy structure, restrained Royal/Cyan support and Yellow/Orange only for intentional actions. The logo should sit on white, off-white or Deep Navy—not on yellow, orange or intense Royal Blue.
