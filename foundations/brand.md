# Brand

Araru is a personal library: editorial, welcoming, quiet, organized and curious. The macaw/Cerrado reference is subtle—an accent, illustration or naming detail—not a theme applied to every screen.


Use “Araru” as the product name, never as a generic noun. Do not redraw or alter the existing logo without a dedicated brand decision. Prefer warm neutrals, a restrained terracotta accent and generous editorial space.
