# Principles

## Content before chrome
Books, covers and reading progress lead the hierarchy. Avoid decorative containers and generic dashboard patterns.


## Calm utility
Make the next useful action obvious, with restrained color and motion.


## Consistent, not identical
Web, Desktop and Android share semantics and personality while respecting platform conventions.


## Respect the reader
Reading is an immersive mode: reduce chrome, preserve position and never hide essential controls from assistive technology.
